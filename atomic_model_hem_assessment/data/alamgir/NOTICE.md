# Published numerical facts, Alamgir / Kan / Lienhard (1980)

Source: https://engines.egr.uh.edu/sites/engines/files/talks/ExperimentalStudyOfDepressurization.pdf
Title: An Experimental Study of the Rapid Depressurization of Hot Water.
Journal of Heat Transfer 102 (1980), 433–438. Table1, printed435 / PDF page3.

This file contains the previously visually checked numerical facts for all four
runs appearing in Figure4. It is not an article copy, raw sensor record or a
digitized time history. No open license for the article has been established;
its figures, pages and text are not redistributed here. No article-wide Apache
or HIPER CC BY-SA license is asserted.

Temperatures are local interpolated initial values; wave speed is one published
rarefaction speed per run, not a separate measurement at each sensor. Input
accuracies ±2% of initial pressure and ±0.6°C come from footnote3, printed434.
The source extract does not establish uncertainty of wave speed, covariance or
confidence level. Comparing bulk-fluid sound speed to a pipe rarefaction speed
is a descriptive screen: pipe compliance, temperature profiles and experimental
wave-speed inference are not represented by the pointwise EOS calculation.
